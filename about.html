About Student Marketplace

Student Marketplace is a campus-focused platform for students at Tshwane University of Technology. Our goal is to make buying and selling textbooks, electronics, furniture and everyday student items simple, local and safer.

We are launching first at TUT to validate the idea, build trust, and create a working marketplace before expanding to other universities across South Africa.
