Safety tips for students

Your safety is more important than any deal.

ℹ️
These tips are active guidance. SafeConnect is planned for a later phase and is not operational yet.

1. Check seller information

Look at the seller’s name and campus. Prefer verified sellers when available.

2. Avoid suspiciously cheap offers

If a price is far below market value, ask for more photos or a video of the item working.

3. Never share passwords or OTPs

No legitimate buyer or seller will ask for your banking password or OTP.

4. Meet in safe public places

Prefer busy campus areas during daytime. Tell a friend where you are going.

5. Inspect before you pay

Test electronics and check textbooks carefully before completing the exchange.

6. Keep evidence

Save chats and, if possible, a photo of the item and any agreement.

7. Report suspicious activity

Stop the transaction and report the listing if something feels wrong.

Future: SafeConnect

A customer-protection process is planned. It is not yet available.
